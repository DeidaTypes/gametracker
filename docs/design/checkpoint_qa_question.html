How it should feel: motion

  • Opening a question: it slides in from the right over a fully drawn Q&A home, and the home shifts left with the same light dim every other screen uses. Covers, title and nav stay on screen the whole time.
  • Back (button or swipe): the exact reverse, and the swipe follows your finger. No dark wash, no covers popping in, no bottom bar snapping in at the end, no title appearing late.
  • First frame is final: the question, best answer and answers are all there when the slide starts. Nothing pops in or pushes content down.
  • Mark as best: the answer glides into the best answer card. Reduced motion makes it instant.
