The flow

  1. Make it yours. Just the themes: two rows of Dark and two of Light. The screen itself is the preview: each tap recolors everything in about a third of a second and the mascot hops. The choice saves the moment they tap.
  2. Where do you play? The chips are gone. Platforms are tiles with line icons that light up in the theme color (Retro added for the handheld and cartridge crowd). Eras became one "since when" rail: tap the decade you started and the bar stretches to today, because nobody stopped playing in the 2000s.
  3. Your 4 favorites. Search on top, the four slots underneath in one row (the same four the profile shows). Type, tap a result: it pops into the next empty slot, the search clears and the cursor stays, so the next search starts right away. Tap a filled slot to take it out. No suggestions, since they haven't picked anything yet.
  4. What are you playing right now? One game only: picking another swaps it. The "On your Home" card shows exactly what they'll see next.
  5. Find your people. Up to four players who share their favorites, each with the reason. Hidden if nobody matches. Then "Let's play" lands on Home.

What goes

  • "Five from your past", and the two words-only intro screens with their glow.
  • Every step keeps Skip. A player who skips everything still lands on Home in the theme they picked.
