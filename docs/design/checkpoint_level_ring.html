The five tiers

Each tier now has its own hue, not just a lighter or darker metal: Bronze is a red copper, Silver a cool steel, Gold a saturated yellow, Platinum a teal and Diamond a blue-to-violet prism (the colors most ranked games use, so players read them instantly). A bright band runs along each ring for a metallic shine, the ring is a touch thicker (3 pt) with a thin dark edge, and the top two keep their effects: Platinum a soft glow, Diamond a glow plus a glint (no glint with Reduce Motion).

What changes

RingFills by progress into the current level and starts over at each new level. 3 pt line with a thin dark edge, 3 pt from the photo. The track is the tier's metal at low strength, so the color reads even right after a level up.
TiersBronze 1–9 (red copper) · Silver 10–19 (cool steel) · Gold 20–29 (yellow gold) · Platinum 30–39 (teal, soft glow) · Diamond 40–50 (blue-violet prism, glow + glint). Each has a dark-theme and a light-theme version. At 50 the Diamond ring stays full.
NamesThe metal replaces the rank names (Rookie, Player, Pro, Elite, Master, Legend). One idea instead of two: "Level 15 · Silver" in the level row and the popup.
Level row and popupTheir rings and the XP bar use your tier's metal.
Level upThe celebration finishes the lap with a quick flash. On a tier change it says so: "Level 10 · Silver ring".
Your photoNo tag. Nothing on the page moves. It still opens Edit Profile. Visitors see your ring the same way.
Two things to know. Bronze is a copper color, which the design system bans everywhere except badge tiers. These rings would be the second exception, and I'd add that to the rules. The Locker's planned ring colors (Cobalt, Mint, Violet, Gold, Prism) would clash with your metal, so the metal stays fixed by level. Locker rings become effects on top of it instead (Glow, a slow turn, a Legend shimmer). I'll redo that reward list when we get to the Locker.
