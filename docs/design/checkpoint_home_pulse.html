What changes in the pulse

  • Person first, for everything that isn't a review. Finishes, lists, questions, sessions and starts start with who did it, a colored verb and when: green for opinions, the theme color for status, purple for lists and questions. They sit on the page separated by thin lines, so the review cards stand out as the main content.
  • Reviews don't change. They keep today's card exactly: cover and title in the box, stars and score, the words, the byline, Like and Reply. The only addition is the Backlog button at the end of the row.
  • Every other moment has its own shape. A finish gets a band of the game's own cover art, blurred behind it. A list shows its covers fanned out. A question shows the question itself, with Answer when you've played that game. Sessions and starts are one quiet line.
  • Playing now. A strip of people in a game right now, with their game's cover on their avatar. It uses the presence the app already tracks for co-op signals. "Your game" appears when they're in the game you're playing. Tapping a person opens their profile. The strip and its "Playing now" label only exist while at least one person is actually in a game: with nobody playing there's no header, no empty circles, no gap. The pulse starts straight with posts. It appears and leaves with the standard content fade as people start and stop.
  • Save from anyone's post. A Backlog button on every game post adds it to your Backlog (Want to Play) and confirms with the approved notice. It turns into "In Backlog" and hides on games already in your library. The feed doubles as discovery.
  • Less repetition. When several people finish the same game in a week, they become one post. Several sessions by one person on one day become one line ("3 sessions today · 4h 20m"). Spoiler reviews stay blurred until tapped.
  • Cleaner. Posts from deleted accounts stay on game pages but leave Home.

What it needs (all existing data)

  • Home starts selecting the activity it already records: sessions, started, lists made, plus the Q&A posts it already shows. Today it shows only written reviews and finishes.
  • Playing now reads the live presence the app already tracks. Backlog uses the existing add-to-library path. Grouping happens on the phone, in the feed it already loaded.
  • No new tables. Cover art comes from the covers already on each post, including the blurred band on finishes.
