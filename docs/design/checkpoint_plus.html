Settings
  • One accent. Cobalt is only on the selected plan, the button, links and the badge. No gradient on this screen.
  • Plans. Yearly is selected by default. "Save 44%" is calculated from the two real prices (12 × monthly vs yearly), and hidden if the math can't be done.
  • Subscription text. Apple requires the price per period, auto-renewal, how to cancel, and Terms and Privacy links on the purchase screen. They come from site.js; if those links aren't configured yet, the whole paywall stays hidden.
  • Success. A centered card that fades in (Oct 24 rule) with the success haptic. Cancelled purchases show nothing. Failures show one red line under the button.
  • Can't load. If the offering can't load (offline, no key), the plans area becomes a quiet retry card. Entry points are hidden entirely when the RevenueCat key is missing.
