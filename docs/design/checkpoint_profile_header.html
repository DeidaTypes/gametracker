Profile header, tightened up

A, rebuilt to hold up however a profile is filled in. All your counts now sit in one row beside the photo, so there's no separate followers line. There's one main button at a time, banners have a proper home, and the extras (best answers, socials, taste match) read as quiet lines instead of pills. The three phones show the same design in three states. The switches change all three at once, including names up to 50 characters, an iPhone SE width, and how it looks while the numbers load.
