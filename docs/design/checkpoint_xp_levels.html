Player Level: XP, levels and the Locker

Everything you already do in Checkpoint earns XP: logging, finishing, reviewing, helping in Q&A, earning badges. XP fills your level. Every level unlocks something for your Locker, and what's in your Locker changes how you look to everyone else: your avatar ring, the title under your name, the frame on your 4 favorites, your app icon. There's nothing to buy. You can only earn it.

Where it lives

No new tab and no change to the Home header. It lives on your Profile, rides inside notices you already get, and shows up on you wherever other people see you.

1Profile: level card

Takes the place of the "Next Milestone" card in your Profile bento, so nothing is added to the page. Level ring, XP bar, and the next reward. Visitors see your level too.

2Level popup

Tap the card: a centered popup (like Streak and Challenge details) with this week's quests, today's XP, recent XP and the reward track.

3The Locker

Where every reward goes. Rings, titles, favorites frames, app icons and Streak Freezes. Tap to equip. Opened from the popup or from Edit Profile.

4Everywhere you appear

Your equipped ring shows on your avatar on Pulse posts, comments, Q&A, Find people and your profile. Your title and level show on your profile.

5Inside notices

No extra pop-ups for XP. The capsule you already get ("Session logged") gains a quiet second line: "+10 XP".

6Level up

The one big moment: the same full-screen celebration as badges, showing your new level and what it just unlocked, with "Equip now".

XP per action

Real playing and writing earn the most. Quick taps earn a little, and each action has a daily cap so nobody can farm a level. Every amount is awarded once per thing (finishing the same game twice doesn't pay twice).

Levels and how long they take

Each level costs 50 XP more than the one before it (100 XP for Level 2, 150 for Level 3, and so on). A regular player earns about 100 XP a day. Six ranks give the number a name.

What every level unlocks

Something every few levels, all of it going to the Locker. Cosmetic only: nothing changes how the app works for anyone else, and nothing can be bought.

The rules that keep it fair

Earned, never bought

No XP for sale, no paid boosts. A level means you actually played. Plus can add extra ring styles later, but never XP.

Never taken away

Deleting a review or a game doesn't remove XP, and a break never lowers your level. Quests just wait for you.

Can't be farmed

Daily caps on quick actions, once-per-thing awards, and XP is added on the server from the same events the badges read, so it can't be faked from the phone.

Fair to people who were here first

On launch day your XP is counted from your whole history (same daily caps). You get one card: "You're Level 14", with everything you unlocked. No pile of popups.
