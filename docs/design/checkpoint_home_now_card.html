One quiet card for what you're playing

Today the top of Home is two cards, three outlined buttons, a brown band, a chip and a ring that says 14 twice. The new version is one card. The game and its main action sit on top, your streak and your year sit underneath, separated by one hairline. Log a session stays one tap away. Finish and Stuck? Ask move into the ⋯ next to it. Every piece of information is still there, and The pulse starts about 65 pt higher. On your iPhone, tap anything in the New screen.

What changed

Two cards become oneNow Playing and the streak / challenge strip share one surface. Your play, your streak and your year read as one thing, because they are: logging feeds the streak, finishing feeds the year.
Three buttons become one, plus ⋯Log a session stays big. Finish and Stuck? Ask live in the ⋯ beside it, a two-tap sheet. Finish is rare and shouldn't sit one stray tap away.
The brown band is goneThe cover's color is now a soft glow from the top-left corner, about a third as strong. Cover 96 → 68 pt so the title gets room.
Less ink in the numbersNo SPARK chip (Spark is colored text). Week dots are small and solid instead of outlined. The challenge ring no longer repeats the 14 inside it.
