X-style transitions

Try it in the phone. Everything here uses one duration (340ms) and one easing curve. There are no fades.

  • Tap a game: it slides in from the right. Home shifts left and dims.
  • Back arrow: the exact reverse.
  • Drag from the left edge of a pushed screen: it follows your finger. Let go early and it snaps back.
  • Tabs: instant. Scroll Home, switch to Library and back, and Home is still where you left it.
  • Comment: a sheet slides up. Drag it down or tap outside to close.

In the real app every screen arrives already laid out (cached content or sized placeholders) before it slides in. That's what removes the hiccup.
