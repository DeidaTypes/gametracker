Eight new themes: 8 dark, 8 light

Four dark and four light, so the picker ends up with eight of each. Arcade is rebuilt so it reads nothing like Purple; Graphite and Pocket are gone. Each new one owns a color no other theme uses: teal, neon pink, chartreuse, black cherry, lilac, eucalyptus, lemon and ink-on-paper. Each is a full palette, the same 25 colors every theme sets, so buttons, accents, stars, notices and the nav all fit the background instead of only the background changing. Every text color passes contrast checks (the table below). There's no orange, amber or copper anywhere. Each phone shows the real Home pieces: Now Playing, streak and challenge, a review, a list post and an error notice.

Paper
  • Ocean (approved). Deep teal with cyan.
  • Arcade (rebuilt). Blue-black base instead of indigo, hot pink, electric aqua, ice blue. Side by side with Purple it's a different animal. Button text is dark so it reads on the pink.
  • Volt (new, replaces Mint). Black with electric chartreuse, blue and violet. No other theme, dark or light, uses yellow-green as its main color, so it can't be mistaken for Dark Green or Ocean: their color lives in the background, Volt's lives in the buttons and highlights.
  • Cherry (new). Black cherry base with rose. Warm and dark, the only dark theme with no blue or purple in its base.
  • Lavender. Light partner to Purple.
  • Sage (new). Eucalyptus and moss, light partner to Dark Green.
  • Lemonade (new, replaces Arctic). Bright lemon with deep indigo buttons and ink. The only yellow in the app, and nowhere near Light Blue: yellow and indigo sit on opposite sides of the color wheel. Cream is a soft beige; Lemonade is unmistakably yellow.
  • Paper (new, replaces Pocket). Warm white with black ink, like a magazine. Game covers carry the color.
  • Picker: two rows of four in Dark, two rows of four in Light. Same in onboarding's first step.

What shipping them touches

  • theme.css: one block per theme (below, ready to paste); Lavender, Sage, Lemonade and Paper join the light-theme rules.
  • themePreview.js: the eight entries and their place in THEME_ORDER (the picker tiles).
  • index.html: the eight background colors in the startup color list, so they never flash on launch.
  • LaunchScreenCache.swift: the eight ids in the known-theme list, the four lights in the light-status-bar list, so the launch picture opens in them too.
  • Everything else in the app already reads these tokens.
