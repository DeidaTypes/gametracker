Profile header · v9 · Nov 3

The player card, with even numbers and the bio under it

The card stays: its edge and shine take your tier's colors. The numbers now sit in one small tray at the bottom of the card. Four equal slots, each number centered over its label, thin dividers between them. Every number lines up the same way no matter how long it is, so 2 and 2.4k look equally at home. The back of the card uses the same tray for your Career numbers. Under the card: your bio, then the buttons on other people's profiles, then the tabs. The Playing card is gone from the header.

Theme
