How it opens

First frameThe iPhone's own launch screen shows the same logo and name in your theme's color, so the first frame and the app's first frame are identical: no jump, no flash.
Signed inLogo and name hold until Home is ready, then fade out while Home fades in (250 ms, the existing fade).
Signed outLogo and name glide up into the sign-in page's brand spot (same size, so it's a pure move) while the form fades in around them.
Size and typeMascot 84 pt tall, 16 pt gap, "Checkpoint" in DM Sans Bold 28 pt, letter-spacing -2%. Light text on dark themes, dark ink on light themes.
Reduce MotionNo glide: the brand fades and the page fades in.
