What changes: the green box becomes a small capsule in your theme's card color, right under the Dynamic Island. A small icon says what happened (green check for a game added, purple for lists, red only when something failed). Games show their cover. Undo sits on the right when there is one.

How it moves: it drops in softly, waits about 2 seconds (4 with Undo), and fades away. A new one replaces the current one instead of piling up.

Words: short, no exclamation marks: "Added to Backlog", "Added to Best of 2026", "List deleted".

Capsule: 44 tall min, fully rounded, theme surface + hairline + soft shadow; 8pt under the island. Lead: 32 circle, 16% tint of the meaning color (green = added/saved, purple = lists/social, neutral = copied/updated, red = failed); or the game's cover (24x32, radius 6). Text: 14.5 semibold title, optional 12.5 muted second line; one line each, ellipsis. Undo: accent text behind a hairline, right side. Tap the toast or swipe it up to dismiss. In: 260, fade + drop 8pt. Out: 220 fade. Reduce Motion: fade only. One at a time: a new toast crossfades over the current one. Errors stay 4s.
