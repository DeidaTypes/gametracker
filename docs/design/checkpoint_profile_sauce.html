Profile header, with sauce

The layout you picked stays. What changes is the feel: the top of every profile is painted with the art of the game that person is playing, or their #1 favorite, blurred into a color field the way a console profile does it. So yours glows Final Fantasy, the next person's glows Batman. Under the bio, a card shows what you're playing. As you scroll, the art drifts slower than the page and the bar frosts over. Scroll inside any phone, or tap Play.

Spec

Art source1) Uploaded banner (as today). 2) Cover of their most recent Currently Playing game. 3) Their #1 favorite. 4) None: the theme wash from PH-1. Same rule on every profile.
Art lookThe cover, enlarged to fill the width, blur 26 pt, saturation 160%, top 262 pt (status bar included). A dark shade at the top so the status bar and icons read in white. Masked to fade into the page by the photo row. On light themes a 6% wash of the page color sits over it so it stays soft.
Photo + counts84 pt photo (tier ring as today, soft shadow) overlapping the art's lower edge; counts bottom-aligned beside it, number 20 pt. 72 pt photo at 359 pt and narrower.
NameName 24 pt bold (20 pt over 24 characters), full width, never cut off. No level line: the level stays in the Level card on the Home tab.
Playing nowOnly when they have a Currently Playing game: a 56 pt frosted row (surface 72% + blur, hairline, radius 14) with a 30×40 cover, "PLAYING NOW" label (review green, 11 pt), title (14.5 semibold, one line) and their hours on it. Pulsing dot while a session is live (still under Reduce Motion). Tap → game page.
ScrollThe art moves at half the scroll speed and fades out over 220 pt. Past the name, the bar fills with the frosted page color + hairline, its icons switch to theme colors, and the display name replaces the @handle. Pull-to-refresh stretches the art from the top. Reduce Motion: no parallax or stretch, the bar still fills.
Everything elseCounts, buttons, extras line, taste match, banner menu and photo menu from PH-1 to PH-3 stay as built.
