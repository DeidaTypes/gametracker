What changes

  • A band, like a finish. 112 pt tall, 14 radius, the same scrim and light text as the Finished band, so lists and finishes read as one family instead of a flat grey box.
  • The list's own art behind it. Up to four of its covers sit side by side, blurred and darkened, so every list gets its own color. A soft wash of the list purple in the top-right corner ties it to the purple "made a list".
  • Posters, fanned. The same three-poster fan as Library's list cards (center on top, the two behind tilted 9°). A two-game list shows two posters, never an empty plate. A list with a custom cover uses that one picture.
  • What's inside. A "List" chip with the layers icon, the name at 17 pt on one line, and up to two lines of "5 games · DOOM Eternal, Hades and 3 more", taken from the list's real first titles.
  • No art yet. Covers missing: a solid band in the second surface color with one placeholder plate, text in the theme's text color.
  • Unchanged. The head line, Like and Reply, tapping opens the list, empty lists still don't post.
