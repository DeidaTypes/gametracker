The fix is one shape for every post: who (avatar, name, what they did, when), what (a card), react (Like, Reply, Backlog). Big moments keep the big band; small moments get a slim card in the same style. Same edges, same spacing, same buttons.

What changes

One headerEvery post opens the same way: the 32 pt avatar (with its level ring) top left, then "name verb · time" on one line. No more small centered avatars.
Slim cardStarts, sessions, your own finishes and the other small moments get a 64 pt card: the cover as a poster on the left, the title, one line of detail, and the cover blurred and darkened behind it, a quieter version of the big band. It lines up exactly with the bands (same left edge, same width, 12 pt corners).
Same buttonsLike and Reply under every post, Backlog on the right when it's a game you could add. Your own posts show your heart count instead of Like.
Clearer details"When" lives in the header (· 25m). The card says what happened in words: "8 hr today · 1 session", "2 sessions · 6 hr · Now playing", "Now playing", "Finished". No more reading "25m" and "8h" side by side.
One post per gameWhen the same person starts a game and logs sessions on it within a day, that's one post ("testing123 started and played Super Mario Odyssey"), not two in a row.
UnchangedThe Finished, List and Review bands, Q&A posts, the Playing Now strip, the order of the feed and what it includes.
