What changes from what's on your phone now

  • The bottom sheet is gone. Ask and Answer use CenteredModal, the popup Write review and Journal already use. That fixes the half-height snap, the header under the status bar, and the drop and re-rise after picking a game.
  • The solid blue input blocks become card-colored fields with a hairline border. The focused field gets a blue border.
  • Topic chips become the review's square Vibe pills. The selected chip is solid blue with white text.
  • The white platform box moves into the game's meta line as tappable blue text with a small chevron.
  • "Where are you?" and "Contains spoilers" move into one grouped box with a hairline between rows, like Liked and Spoilers on Write review.
  • After posting: the popup fades out, the question opens, and "Question posted" uses the app's standard toast position.
