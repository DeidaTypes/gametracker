Help with Checkpoint.

Contact us

Email

In the app, Settings → Help & about → Send feedback opens an email that already includes your app version and device, which helps us fix problems faster.

Delete your account

  1. Open Checkpoint and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Account, then Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is removed and what stays.

Report content or a user

Open the ⋯ menu on a review, comment, message, profile, list, question or answer and choose Report. Pick a reason and add details if you like. We review every report within 24 hours and remove content and users that break the Terms of Use.

Block someone

Open their profile, tap ⋯ and choose Block. You can review or undo blocks in Settings → Privacy & safety → Blocked users.

Change your email or password

Settings → Account. If you forgot your password, use Forgot password on the sign-in screen.

Control who sees your activity

Settings → Privacy & safety lets you choose who can message you and who can see your activity.
