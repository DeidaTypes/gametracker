Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Checkpoint is a game library and tracking app. This page explains what the app collects, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • You create an account with an email address and password. Your email is never shown on your profile.
  • What you add to Checkpoint (your library, ratings, reviews, lists, questions and so on) is stored with our backend provider, Supabase.
  • Some of it is public, some of it is private. The list below says which.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can delete your account inside the app. See Deleting your account for what that removes and what it does not.

What we collect

  • Email address and password

    Used to sign in and to reset your password. The password is stored only as a hash by our authentication provider; we never see it.

    Who can see it
    Only you and us. Never shown in the app.

  • Username, display name, bio

    Your profile.

    Who can see it
    Public. Anyone who opens your profile, including people without an account who open a shared profile link.

  • Profile photo and banner

    Images you choose and upload. The app asks for photo access only when you pick one.

    Who can see it
    Public, with your profile.

  • Favorite games, showcase badges, current obsessions, genre and platform badges

    Optional profile content.

    Who can see it
    Public, with your profile.

  • Game library

    Each game's status (Backlog, Currently Playing, Played or Dropped), your star ratings, and play sessions with hours played.

    Who can see it
    Others, as set by your “See my activity” setting (everyone, followers or only you). You always see your own.

  • Reviews

    Reviews you write, plus likes and comments on them.

    Who can see it
    Readable by anyone who has the link, including people without an account, subject to your “See my activity” setting and to blocks.

  • Lists

    Lists you create, the games in them, collaborators, saves and list comments.

    Who can see it
    Public lists: signed-in users. Private lists: you and the collaborators you add.

  • Journal entries

    Private notes about games you play.

    Who can see it
    Only you. Never public and never shared with followers.

  • Comments, likes and reactions

    What you post on reviews, lists and activity.

    Who can see it
    Anyone who can see the content they are attached to.

  • Messages

    Direct messages to other users.

    Who can see it
    Only you and the other person. You choose who can message you in Settings.

  • Questions and answers

    Community Q&A: questions, answers, “helpful” votes and “stuck here too” taps.

    Who can see it
    Signed-in users. Not visible to people without an account.

  • Follows

    Who you follow and who follows you.

    Who can see it
    Shown on profiles, subject to blocks.

  • Badges, streaks and goals

    Progress the app tracks from your activity.

    Who can see it
    Badges you showcase are public. Streaks and goals follow your activity setting.

  • Taste and recommendations

    Signals from your library and your swipes, used to suggest games.

    Who can see it
    Only you.

  • Notifications

    Events such as new followers and replies.

    Who can see it
    Only you.

  • Blocks

    People you have blocked.

    Who can see it
    Only you.

  • Reports

    When you report something: what you reported, the reason, and any details you add.

    Who can see it
    Only you and the people who review reports. The person you reported is not told who reported them.

  • Invites

    If someone joins from your invite link, we record that you invited them.

    Who can see it
    Only you.

  • Presence (“playing now”)

    Only if you turn it on in Settings.

    Who can see it
    Other users, while it is on.

Device and app information

Checkpoint does not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifier or browsing history, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

  • Feedback emails. When you tap Send feedback, the email that opens is pre-filled with the app version and build, your iOS version, your device model and your account ID, so we can reproduce problems. You can read and edit it before sending, and nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • On your device. The app stores settings (such as theme and accessibility options) and cached copies of your library on your device so it works quickly. Signing out or deleting the app removes them.
  • Crash reports. Checkpoint will send crash reports (what failed, app version, iOS version and device model) so we can fix bugs. They do not include your reviews, messages or library. Draft note: crash reporting ships after Sprint 1. Delete this item if it does not ship.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sign you in, show your library, and show your public content to others the way you set it up.
  • To personalize game recommendations from your own activity.
  • To keep the community safe: reports are reviewed and content or accounts that break the Terms of Use are removed.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who else is involved

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in, file storage (profile photos and banners) and server functions. All of the data above is stored there.
  • IGDB (a Twitch service) supplies game information such as titles, covers and release dates. The app asks for it through our own server, which keeps our IGDB credentials private, so your account details are not sent. The text of a game search is passed on to IGDB, and cover images load from IGDB's image servers, which can see your IP address as any web server can.
  • Have I Been Pwned is used to check a new password against known breaches. Only the first five characters of a hash of the password are sent, never the password itself.
  • Apple distributes the app through the App Store. The share sheet and the mail app you use for feedback are Apple's and run on your device.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete your content. You can edit your profile and delete your reviews, lists, comments and questions in the app.
  • Privacy settings. Settings → Privacy & safety lets you choose who can message you and who can see your activity.
  • Blocking. Settings → Privacy & safety → Blocked users. Blocked people cannot see your profile, message you or find you in search.
  • Email and password. Settings → Account.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account and confirm. Deletion is immediate and final. There is no recovery period.

What is removed right away:

  • Your library and play sessions, journal entries, taste and recommendation data, badges, streaks and goals.
  • Your messages, blocks, follows, likes, reactions, saves, pins and notifications.
  • Your name, username, bio, profile photo, banner, favorite games and showcase badges. Your profile becomes “[deleted user]”.
  • Your ability to sign in. The account is locked and cannot be used again.

What stays:

  • Reviews, lists, comments and Q&A you wrote remain so other people's conversations are not broken, but they are no longer linked to a name or profile. If you want any of them gone, delete them yourself before you delete your account.
  • Reports you filed and reports about you, which we keep for safety.
  • Your email address stays in our sign-in provider's records in a locked state, so it can no longer be used to sign in. To have that record erased as well, email us at the address below.

Children

Checkpoint is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and tell you in the app.
